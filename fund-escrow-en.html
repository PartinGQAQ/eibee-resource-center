The Trust Gap in Influencer Marketing

In in-depth conversations with more than 500 brands, the following five pain points came up again and again:

💸

Payment Without Delivery

Creators go silent after the advance payment lands, drag out drafts, and delay publishing indefinitely — leaving brands with no way to recover their money

📉

Uncontrolled Delivery Quality

Content drifts off-brief, production is sloppy, brand tone is off — and acceptance reviews turn into endless disputes

🌐

Cross-Border Payment Friction

Multi-currency settlement, cross-border compliance, FX losses, and long, opaque payment cycles

🎭

Fraudulent Metrics

Bought followers, inflated views and fake engagement — brands pay for phantom impressions while real ROI becomes impossible to verify

🔀

Unclear Accountability

Brands, agencies and creators collaborate across multiple parties; responsibility boundaries are blurry, and when things go wrong, everyone points elsewhere

02

Why It Was Never Solved?

Gap 01

No Fund Security Mechanism

In the traditional model, brands pay creators directly; once the money leaves, control is gone. No escrow in between, no milestone-based release, no link to acceptance.

Gap 02

No Process Transparency

Brands often only see a report after the campaign ends. Communication, revisions, publishing cadence and performance data during execution remain a black box.

Gap 03

No Forward-Looking Risk Control

Risks like follower loss, policy violations and missed KPIs are typically discovered only after the fact — with no real-time monitoring or early-warning mechanisms.

03

Four Protection Mechanisms

Mechanism 1 · FUND ESCROW

Fund Escrow — Every Dollar Released Against Delivery Milestones

Brand budgets go into an Eibee escrow account rather than being paid directly to creators. Funds are released in stages against predefined delivery milestones:

1

Task Confirmation

Once the creator accepts the invitation and confirms the task requirements, a portion of the funds is released to secure the collaboration kickoff

2

Content Delivery

Once the creator submits a first draft and it passes brand review, the next stage of funds is released

3

Publishing & Acceptance

Once the content is published as required and performance meets the agreed standards, the remaining funds are released

If a creator fails to deliver as agreed, the brand can refuse the release of funds, and the platform arbitrates under its rules.

Mechanism 2 · CROSS-BORDER PAYMENT

Cross-Border Payment — Pay Creators Worldwide with One-Stop Compliant Settlement

  • Multi-currency support: pay in the currency matching each creator's region to reduce FX losses
  • Direct cross-region transfers: funds flow from the platform escrow account straight to the creator's receiving account
  • Trackable payment status: progress, status and arrival time of every payment, visible in real time
  • Package orders and invoice management: standardized, auditable corporate finance workflows
  • Corporate email configuration: ensures fund notifications and billing information are delivered securely
Mechanism 3 · TRANSPARENT DELIVERY

Transparent Delivery — From Black Box to Full Visibility

The entire pipeline — campaign creation, task assignment, creator outreach, content review, publishing tracking — all in one workspace:

Content Delivery Workflow

Risk Alert Center — Spot Problems Early, Don't Pay for Them Later

Creator Anomaly Monitoring

KPI Risk Prediction

Predicts achievement probability and warns early when it drops below threshold

Platform Governance: Clear Rights and Responsibilities

Eibee breaks influencer marketing into a standardized pipeline — "campaign → task → invitation → content review → publishing tracking" — with a clear owner and delivery standard for every step.

Agency Management Mechanism: Delegation Without Loss of Control

Agencies handle day-to-day operational execution, while brands retain full visibility — they can view progress, creator lists, data and risk alerts, but cannot directly modify agency-managed campaigns. This preserves the agency's operational autonomy while the brand keeps the right to know and to supervise.

AI agents run through the entire workflow to boost efficiency, but key decisions — acceptance criteria, fund release, risk handling — always remain with the brand. AI is an assistant, not a decision-maker.
