Strategy Is Not Guesswork

Influencer marketing involves too many decision variables: market opportunity, audience profiles, platform selection, content direction, creator mix, budget allocation, campaign scheduling, KPI setting, contingency planning, and review metrics — if any one of these 10 modules is decided by "gut feeling," the entire campaign can go off track.

In Eibee, once a brand enters its basic information, the system automatically initializes the brand profile, then the AI strategy engine streams out a complete strategy report covering all 10 modules above. You can also give instructions in natural language:

"Create a Q3 campaign plan for wireless earbuds targeting Gen Z in Southeast Asia, with a $50K budget"

The system generates a structured plan right away. The same complete campaign strategy takes a senior marketing manager 2–3 days on average; an AI-generated first draft takes just minutes.

One principle: strategy can be generated fast, but it must exist. Think it through before you act — that matters more than anything else.

Creator Matching Is Not Follower Ranking

Finding the "right person" is a hundred times harder than finding a "big one." In the early days, when we helped brands find creators, screening hundreds of profiles for a single campaign often took the team one to two weeks. What was even more critical was the quality of judgment:

📈

Inflated Metrics

Follower counts look impressive, engagement rates are absurdly low, and fake-traffic traces are obvious

🎭

Audience Mismatch

The creator covers beauty, but the followers are mostly middle-aged men — spending on them is money down the drain

⚠️

Brand Safety

Past statements or content tone clash with brand values, triggering PR crises

🕳️

Value-for-Money Trap

Top-tier creators charge high without guaranteed conversion, while high-potential mid-tier creators go unnoticed

Eibee breaks the creator profile into six core dimensions — follower quality, engagement rate, content focus, audience match, past collaboration performance, and value for money — and scores them comprehensively. Brands can describe their needs in a single sentence, and AI semantic search directly surfaces matching creators.

Lesson learned: The precision of creator matching determines the ceiling of campaign performance. The right creator can deliver 10x the results of the wrong one.

Risk Management Is Not After-the-Fact Firefighting

Risks in influencer marketing don't appear only when they erupt — they're always there; you just don't see them. In cross-border scenarios, time zones, language, and cultural differences already make problem response slow.

MONITORING

Creator Anomaly Monitoring

Continuously tracks follower changes, data fluctuations, and public sentiment — alerting you at the first sign of abnormal follower loss, sudden engagement drops, or negative statements.

CONTENT

Content Anomaly Monitoring

Content submitted and published by creators is monitored; the system automatically identifies violations, off-brief work, and brand safety issues.

PREDICTION

KPI Achievement Risk Prediction

Based on real-time data and historical performance curves, it predicts the probability of hitting final KPIs and offers adjustment recommendations before targets fall short.

The highest level of risk management is preventing risk from happening at all. This isn't a slogan — it's a practical capability built on data and algorithms.
05

Fund Security & Delivery Standards

Cross-border influencer marketing involves international payments, and the more fundamental issue is trust — brands fear paying upfront without delivery, while creators fear delivering and never getting paid.

Eibee supports escrow: the brand's budget goes into an escrow account first, and funds are released only after the creator completes the agreed deliverables and the brand confirms. If the creator fails to deliver as agreed, the brand can get a refund. Meanwhile, the platform supports cross-border payments, sparing brands the cost of integrating different countries' currencies and payment channels.

Delivery standards are equally transparent — each task's deliverable requirements, review criteria, and revision rounds are clearly defined within the platform, eliminating the inefficient back-and-forth of "I thought you wanted A, you thought I understood B."

9 years, 500+ brands, 10,000+ campaigns, 30+ countries — behind these numbers is our ongoing exploration of moving influencer marketing from a "handcraft workshop" to "industrialized operations."

Some brand clients have already achieved a 2.5x ROI uplift. That's not the credit of AI alone — it's the result of "AI capabilities + human judgment + systematic processes" working together.
