What Is Eibee

Eibee Agent OS is an AI influencer-marketing intelligence workspace built for brands expanding globally. It turns work that once required an entire marketing team — strategy, creators, content, data, and risk control — into a system driven by collaborating AI Agents: from market insight, strategy generation, influencer matching, and task execution to performance tracking, risk alerts, and post-campaign review, all as a single closed loop across the full chain.

Drawing on 9 years of accumulated influencer-marketing expertise, we train our AI to understand the real relationships among brand, creator, content, and audience — so overseas growth moves away from "gut feel and guesswork" toward certainty that is computable, repeatable, and attributable.

In one sentence: Eibee is your AI influencer-marketing team — online 24/7, collaborating end to end from the first brief to the final review.

Core Capability Map

Eibee is built on four AI engines that cover the full influencer-marketing lifecycle, with real-time AI support at every stage:

ENGINE 01

AI Strategy Engine

Brand profiles initialize automatically, and 10 modules generate a marketing strategy report in streaming form. Accepts natural-language input and produces an executable plan in minutes.

  • Market opportunities, audience insights, platform selection
  • Content direction, influencer mix, budget allocation
  • Timeline, KPI setting, risk contingency plans
ENGINE 02

Intelligent Influencer Matching

AI semantic search plus multi-dimensional profile scoring automatically surfaces hidden problems such as inflated metrics, mismatched audiences, and brand-safety risks.

  • Describe your need in one sentence and retrieve the right people by meaning
  • Holistic assessment of audience quality, engagement rate, and content fit
  • High-risk influencers flagged automatically before any collaboration
ENGINE 03

End-to-End Task Management

Campaign creation, invitations, communication, review, and post-publishing tracking run on one system. AI-personalized outreach keeps every party aligned and transparent.

  • Smart Outreach for higher reply rates
  • Brands, agencies, and creators on the same workspace
  • Transparent progress with clear ownership
ENGINE 04

Risk Alert Center

Real-time alerts on influencer anomalies, content anomalies, and KPI shortfall risks — paired with funds escrow to protect every dollar of budget.

  • Alerts for follower loss, metric anomalies, and negative sentiment
  • Automatic detection of violations, off-brief content, and brand-safety issues
  • Early warning when KPIs are at risk of being missed
